## Pricing: start with real comps, not a gut feeling

The single biggest mistake I see sellers make is anchoring to what a neighbor's house sold for two years ago. Carroll County's market moves month to month, not year to year. A real comparative market analysis looks at what's actually closed in the last 30-60 days, adjusted for condition, lot size, and updates - not a Zillow estimate.

## What buyers actually judge before they ever call

Buyers decide whether to request a showing almost entirely from three things: the front-of-house photo, the price relative to similar active listings, and how long it's been sitting. Everything else - staging, listing description, even most interior photos - matters far less than people assume.

## A realistic timeline

From the day we agree on a price to a signed contract, well-priced homes in this market are typically moving in 2-4 weeks. That number swings hard based on price band and condition, which is exactly why a real conversation about your specific property matters more than a generic number.

## What's worth fixing, and what isn't

Cosmetic issues that show up in the front-of-house photo (siding, walkway, obvious deferred maintenance) are worth addressing. Interior updates that buyers won't see until a showing - once they've already decided to come look - are far lower priority, and often not worth the cost before selling.
